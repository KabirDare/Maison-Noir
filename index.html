Our Story

A quiet room,
a loud conviction.

Maison Noir opened on Altamount Road in 2016 with a single idea: that Mumbai deserved a dining room built entirely around restraint. No spectacle, no shortcuts — just classical French method applied with total devotion to the produce of this coast.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen has been reduced, not added to. We source from six family farms across Maharashtra and Kerala, work in a single seasonal menu, and seat no more than thirty-two guests a night.

Meet the Chef

Vihaan Rao

Executive Chef & Founder

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris and seasoned across three Michelin-starred kitchens in France, Chef Vihaan Rao returned to Mumbai to build a menu that speaks in his mother tongue with a French accent.

"I don't cook French food in India, and I don't cook Indian food dressed as French. I cook the coast I grew up on, using the discipline I was trained in. The two were never really in conflict."
